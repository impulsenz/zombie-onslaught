        How it works

        Survive the night. Machines only attack after dusk. Spend the day mining, building and charging batteries - at night every turret shot runs on stored energy.

        The dome. Your HQ's shield bubble holds the air and stops machines at its shell. Keep the HQ powered so it recharges (blue = healthy, amber < 40%, red < 10%). Build only inside it. Upgrade it at the HQ (Q).

        Oxygen. Your suit refills inside the dome, at any powered lift stop and at O₂ stations. Out in the vacuum and underground it drains.

        Mining. Hold RMB to fire the mining laser straight ahead - it cuts rock and burns machines. Ore fills your backpack and unloads automatically at the HQ or a lift.

        Going deeper. Press F at a lift to pick a level or pay to bore the shaft deeper. Better ore, ruins and ancient modules wait below.

        Power. Wire buildings with V. Solar panels track the sun; batteries carry you through the night. The power panel graphs supply against what you are drawing.
